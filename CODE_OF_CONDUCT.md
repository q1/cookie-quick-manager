# Code of conduct

Cookie Loom welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. In issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces:

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or implementation, not a person.
- Assume questions are asked in good faith and make room for newcomers.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information,
  cookie data, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexualized or hateful content.
- Accept moderation decisions and avoid repeatedly disrupting a discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, limit
participation, or block accounts. They will consider the impact and context of
the behavior and apply these expectations to their own participation too.

Report abusive content using GitHub's **Report content** action. For a private
project-specific concern, use a contact method a maintainer has made available
on their GitHub profile. Avoid public issues containing personal allegations
or identifying details. Platform safety concerns can also be reported through
[GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
